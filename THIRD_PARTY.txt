Сторонние компоненты / Third-party notices
=========================================

FFmpeg is not included in the initial archive.
The application downloads the Windows 'release essentials' archive at:
https://www.gyan.dev/ffmpeg/builds/ffmpeg-release-essentials.zip

The project's official download page links to these Windows builds:
https://ffmpeg.org/download.html

The gyan.dev builds are licensed under GPLv3 (according to the build site).
Before redistributing a copy of the folder that contains downloaded FFmpeg
binaries, please review the applicable GPLv3 requirements, include the
necessary notices, and provide the corresponding source/offer as required.
FFmpeg licensing information: https://ffmpeg.org/legal.html
Gyan.dev builds and source pointers: https://www.gyan.dev/ffmpeg/builds/

This custom GUI and downloader are provided as editable source files. They
invoke FFmpeg as an external program.
